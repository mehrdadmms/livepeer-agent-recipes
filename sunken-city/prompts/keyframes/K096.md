# K096 (hook reference; not a join) - splash on lens
Purpose: 4.0 s hook frame: the white crown of water exploding into the lens with the boy's dark shape inside it. Edit of B1 (source).
Camera: phone-like wide lens 0.4 m above the planks and ~1 m behind the rail at the moment the lens is hit by water; lens partially wetted.
Composition: crown of water fills lower/centre, boy a dark curled shape inside it, Ferris wheel blurred behind, rail and lamp cropped.
Lighting: low orange sun behind/right; droplets backlit, water white-gold, flare.
Compact Prompt:
Candid phone video-still at the instant a small boy cannonballs off the side of a weathered Victorian pier beside the lens: a white, ragged crown of sea water erupts into the camera from below, drops and streaks across the glass, the boy's dark curled shape and a striped sleeve glimpsed inside the spray, his face hidden, the cream iron railing and one lamp post cropped at the frame edge, the Ferris wheel and domed pavilions blurred behind against a low orange sun.
