# 01 Diving into the city (sunken city) — v2 plan
duration_s: 24
## timeline
- 0.0-4.0  ACTION: kid (back view, silhouette) sprints down the weathered pier at golden hour, arms pumping; camera chases low and close behind, planks streaming past, sun flaring ahead
- 4.0      HOOK: cannonball hits the water at exactly 4.0 s (frame 96), splash slams the lens, music hit + splash SFX on the same frame
- 4.0-12.0 PAYOFF: camera follows through the waterline into a bubble cloud, then swims down; the sunken city appears below, lit windows and street lights still on, kid a dark shape sinking beside the lens
- 12.0-20.0 ESCALATION: dive along the drowned street at speed: facades, balconies, awnings, cars on flat tyres, a bus, kelp, fish school crossing frame, god rays, then the street tips into a plaza and the camera banks toward one lit window
- 20.0-24.0 ENDING: camera glides to the lit window, glass fills the frame, something inside is revealed (see options), hold on it, music resolves
## camera
- rig: handheld chase (0-4 s), then gimbal-smooth swim / FPV dive underwater with slight sway
- lens: 18 mm on a 36 mm sensor (about 90 deg vertical), f/2.4, shutter 180 deg, DOF on the kid until the dive, then deep focus
- moves: 0-4 s run 4.5 m/s, height 0.4 m behind and slightly left of kid, shake + step bob, accelerates into the jump
- moves: 4.0-4.5 s impact kick, splash on lens, waterline crossing in one move
- moves: 4.5-12 s descend at about 3 m/s, looking down, then levelling toward the street
- moves: 12-20 s forward dive along the street at 6-8 m/s, weaving between cars and lamp posts, banking at the plaza
- moves: 20-24 s decelerate to 1 m/s, push in on the window, final slow settle
## elements
- characters: kid 8-10 y/o, back view only, dark hoodie and shorts, no face shown, silhouette against the sun; identity refs not needed (silhouette only); ending option B adds a second figure inside the window (silhouette, ref needed only if a face is shown: not planned)
- props: wooden pier, mooring posts, rope coil, lamp posts, traffic light, street signs (invented names), cars, bus, kelp, fish schools, bubbles, marine snow
- environment: golden-hour sea and pier; drowned street canyon with balconied facades, shop awnings, silt drifts, barnacles, algae
## lighting
- above water: low sun ahead-left, warm gold, long planks shadows toward camera, sun flare, thin haze on the horizon
- below water: blue-teal absorption darkening with depth, god rays from the surface, caustic web on the street, warm orange window and street-lamp glow bloomed through the murk, drifting particulate
## music
- reference track: "Levels", Avicii (structure only: build, near-silence, drop)
- brief: original instrumental house / cinematic, 126 BPM, warm pad and pluck under the run with a snare-roll riser, 2-3 frames of near-silence, full-band hit at 4.0 s, driving four-on-the-floor groove under the dive, filtered and floaty at the reveal, final chord resolves at 24 s
## sfx
- 0.0 wooden footfalls and breath, distant waves, gulls
- 2.5 rising whoosh and wind, footfalls accelerate
- 3.9 plank creak then takeoff thump
- 4.0 HIT: deep sub boom + big cannonball splash
- 4.3 underwater rush, muffled roar, bubble fizz
- 8.0 sonar-like low drone, distant creaks of metal
- 12.0 fish school flutter passing, car-body groan
- 16.0 whoosh past bus, rising bubble streams
- 19.5 light hum builds as the window nears
- 20.5 glass tap / muffled reveal sting
- 24.0 final low resolve tone
