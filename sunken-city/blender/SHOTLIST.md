# SEEDANCE_v2 handoff - 01 Diving into the city (24 s, 9:16, previs v3)
The previs is layout, camera and timing only; the look comes from the generated masters in refs/masters/.

## 1. Shot summary
One continuous handheld-to-FPV move. A blond barefoot boy in a navy/white striped tee and rolled jeans sprints down the long timber deck of a Victorian seaside amusement pier toward a 30 m Ferris wheel and domed pavilions silhouetted against a low orange sunset under a deep-blue sky, wet sand beach behind. A low camera chases him. At exactly 4.0 s he vaults the side rail near the far end (wheel looming above) and drops into the sea; the splash bursts and hits the lens. The camera follows through the waterline into a bubble cloud (the boy a dark shape sinking beside the lens), swims down, and emerges into a drowned classical forum in clear blue water with strong god rays: fluted colonnades, arches, statues on plinths, broken column drums, flagstone plaza with sand drifts, a temple with triangular pediment and steps. Warm amber light glows from bronze lanterns, the temple doorways and window. The dive crosses the forum between columns, past a statue, under an arch, up the temple steps into the portico toward the lit window, where a silhouette turns and waves (last 2 s). Tone: wonder.
