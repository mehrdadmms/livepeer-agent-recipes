# sunken-city

A 24 s vertical hook: a boy sprints down a Victorian pier at sunset, vaults the rail at exactly 4.0 s, and the camera dives through a drowned Athens to a lit temple window where a silhouette waves. Blender blocked the camera and timing; Livepeer (GPT Image 2 for the sheets and keyframes, Seedance 2.5 for the take) made the picture.

`outputs/blender_vs_livepeer.mp4` is the side-by-side. `SKILL.md` is the runbook.
