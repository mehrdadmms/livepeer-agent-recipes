# impossible-kick

A 30 s vertical hook: a street footballer juggles a dented can in a back alley and hammers it at exactly 4.0 s; the camera rides the spinning can over the rooftops, through a window, down a football-mad mural street, and the street itself turns into a stadium: balconies and rooftops packed, floodlights warming, a goal across the far end. Blender blocked the camera and timing; Livepeer (GPT Image 2 sheets and keyframes, Seedance 2.5 clips) made the picture.

`outputs/blender_vs_livepeer.mp4` is the side-by-side. `SKILL.md` is the runbook.
